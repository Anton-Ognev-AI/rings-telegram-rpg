## Delivered

- Forward migration `202607150014_tutorial_starter.sql` adds deny-by-default normalized tutorial,
  stat, equipment, ring, offer and profile-action state. The feature flag is disabled by the
  migration and enabled only by the local synthetic seed.
- `progression-v1` pins the teacher, rescue, stat-price, vitality, item and blue-ring constants and
  protects the active payload/hash from mutation.
- The first two canonical runs receive immutable tutorial assignments. Tutorial run 1 supports one
  server-validated early lethal rescue; terminal and eligible three-result expiry paths credit once.
  Start-only, short expiry and explicit abandon do not credit.
- An uncredited tutorial can be retried without consuming its ordinal. At most one attempt for each
  tutorial ordinal can be credited. A new-day start also expires and credits an eligible old
  tutorial before selecting the next state, so scheduler delay cannot skip the training decision.
- The first credit grants 20 cap-subject XP once. The second creates one ordered item offer followed
  by one four-way starter-ring offer.
- Opaque `pa_` HMAC callbacks are bounded to 64 bytes and bind actor, profile version, message,
  action and context. Run and profile actions share one Telegram update lock and cannot both mutate.
- Stat purchase/defer, item accept/discard, ring selection and ring mastery are atomic and replay
  safe. There is no inventory: accept replaces one slot and discard gives no XP.
- The initial stat choice is persisted. Vitality gives +4 max HP and every third purchased vitality
  gives +1 defense. Ring selection installs one compatible ordinary main item, completes tutorial
  `2/2`, grants rank `novice`, and mastery costs 20 XP per +1% without changing the fixed 15% blue
  combat modifier.
- The upgrade verifier rejects remote configuration, applies migration 014 in place over a local
  migration-013 fixture, proves legacy player/config preservation and lazy initialization, and
  restores the full local schema in `finally`.

## Council Corrections

The five-role Gate 4.1 review found and closed three issues before approval:

1. A unique `(player, tutorial_ordinal)` constraint prevented retry after abandon or short expiry.
   It is now a partial unique credit constraint, allowing multiple uncredited attempts but one
   credited completion.
2. A delayed day scheduler could let a new start race ahead of eligible tutorial credit. The
   player-scoped start path now expires and credits the old tutorial first.
3. Nonpositive Telegram update/message IDs could reach a database constraint exception. Both v2
   action namespaces now reject them as `invalid_binding` with zero mutation.

Final Gate 4.1 council verdict: `APPROVE`. The canonical equipment/ring combat projection, player
breakdown, cards and Telegram router remain explicitly in Gate 4.2 and are not claimed here.
