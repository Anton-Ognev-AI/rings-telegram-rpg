## Council Review

Verdict: `APPROVE_IMPLEMENTATION_WITH_VERIFICATION_HOLD`; Phase 4A is not approved.

1. **Player experience:** the flow now shows a meaningful reward after each tutorial day, proves
   the stat affects only a later snapshot and makes both keep/discard decisions lead forward.
2. **Game systems:** the deterministic matrix satisfies the approved rule that no one ring strictly
   dominates all others across every policy and party mode. It also exposes a product debt: on the
   single locked fallback dungeon, fire is pairwise weaker than the other starter builds in the
   reported metric set. This may be a content-affinity artifact, but it must be resolved or tested
   across multiple dungeon archetypes before a player-facing balance claim.
3. **Reliability:** handler/worker restarts, replay, competing stale actions, exact credit/result
   reconciliation and cleanup-on-failure are represented. Runtime evidence remains blocking.
4. **Security/privacy:** remote DB/link overrides fail closed; telemetry is disabled for CLI steps;
   fixture safety is executable; no real identity or secret is stored in the new tests.
5. **Architecture/scope:** Gate 4.3 adds tests and verification tooling, not a parallel gameplay
   implementation. Historical `verify:phase3`, locked migrations 001–013 and locked resolver/content
   files remain unchanged.
